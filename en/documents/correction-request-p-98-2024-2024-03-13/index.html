Request to correct the appeal instruction in ruling P.98/2024 — 13 March 2024

Counsel requests urgent correction of the appeal instruction, referral to the Basic Court and delivery of a certified copy. The submission and court receipt stamp are dated 13 March 2024.

Higher Court in Novi Sad 2024-03-13
This submission of 13 March 2024 asks the Higher Court in Novi Sad to correct the appeal instruction in its ruling P.98/2024 of 26 February 2024. It states that counsel received that ruling on 4 March and repeats the filing of the claim and evidence-preservation motion on 22 February. The submission calls the appeal instruction an oversight and an error. It quotes Article 17, paragraph 4 of the Civil Procedure Law as its argument that an appeal is not permitted, although the court's earlier instruction gave 15 days to appeal. Counsel continues to assert that the Higher Court has subject-matter and territorial jurisdiction, while requesting urgent correction, transfer of the file to the Basic Court and delivery of a certified copy of the correction ruling. These are the submission's arguments and requests; this source does not record a court decision granting them.

Article 17 · Subject-matter jurisdiction

Civil Procedure Act of the Republic of Serbia

The 13 March submission challenges the appeal instruction in Biljana Lepotić’s transfer ruling. It quotes Article 17(4), which bars an appeal when the higher first-instance court transfers a case to a lower court of the same type, and seeks correction so that the transfer does not wait on an unavailable remedy.

Short quotation from the source

… by the Court cannot be lodged, as such an appeal is inadmissible. Why this source is linked
Related source

The 29 April ruling recounts the correction request of 13 March and the jurisdiction objection of 1 April. In its account of the plaintiff’s submissions, it records his statements that the request remained unanswered and that 20 March had been entered as the wrong date of finality. The original 13 March request permits comparison with this subsequent court account.

On 13 March 2024, the plaintiff, through his authorized representative, sent a submission to the Higher Court in Novi Sad requesting that it issue a decision correcting its ruling No. P.98/2024 of 26 February 2024 in the part concerning the instruction on legal remedies, by establishing that no appeal against that ruling was permitted and referring the case file to the Basic Court in Novi Sad, and that it serve the plaintiff’s authorized representative with a certified copy of the ruling correcting ruling No. P.98/2024 of 26 February 2024.

On 1 April 2024, the plaintiff, through his authorized representative, filed a submission with the Basic Court in Novi Sad in which he stated that he was raising a procedural objection to the Basic Court’s lack of subject-matter jurisdiction. He stated that, in its ruling No. P.98/2024 of 26 February 2024 declaring itself without subject-matter jurisdiction, the Higher Court in Novi Sad had incorrectly instructed the plaintiff that he had a right to appeal against that ruling to the Appellate Court in Novi Sad within 15 days of receiving it, whereas such an appeal was in fact not permitted under Article 17(4) of the Civil Procedure Act.

He stated that, on 13 March 2024, the plaintiff’s authorized representative had sent a submission to the Higher Court in Novi Sad requesting a ruling correcting the ruling on lack of jurisdiction, establishing that the court had made an oversight and that no appeal was permitted, but that the Higher Court had not answered that submission.

He then pointed out that the Higher Court had marked an incorrect date of finality for that ruling. He stated that, instead of 26 February 2024 as the day on which the ruling was issued, the Higher Court had determined 20 March 2024 as the day of finality, the day after expiry of an appeal period which could not even have begun to run because an appeal was prohibited by law.
