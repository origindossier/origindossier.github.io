Civil register record / Primary source

Civil register after second adoption - Petrović Milan as sealed second layer

The civil-register extract after the second adoption seals Petrović Milan as the operative layer. It does not restore origin; it carries forward the chain Milivoj Miletić -> Vasić Milan -> Petrović Milan.

Stari Grad Municipality / Novi Sad Civil Registry Office 1984-09-26
The register extract is the final administrative seal of the 1984 loop. It does not restore Milivoj Miletić, Cvita Miletić, or Pavle / Pal Lakatoš. It records Petrović Milan while the Vasić parental frame remains in force. Under Articles 21 and 22 the adoption record moves into the civil register; under Article 24 ordinary adoption does not erase blood kinship. The paper therefore shows the operative result: the Vasić construction is not corrected, but carried forward into the Petrović construction.
