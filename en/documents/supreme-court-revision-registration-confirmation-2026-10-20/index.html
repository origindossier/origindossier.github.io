Supreme Court confirmation of registration as Rev 6599/26 and forwarding of the submission to the reporting judge - 20 October 2026

Reply from Milanka Ranković, Deputy Head of the Court Registry, expressly refers to the inquiry of 18 October 2026. It confirms that the appeal on points of law is registered as Rev 6599/26 and that the author’s submission has been forwarded to the reporting judge for information. This is an administrative notification, not a decision on the merits.

Supreme Court 2026-10-20
On 20 October 2026, Milanka Ranković, signing as Deputy Head of the Court Registry, replies to the author’s request of 18 October. Citing Article 223(3) of the Court Rules of Procedure, the reply confirms that the appeal on points of law against the Court of Appeal in Novi Sad decision Gž2 211/26, connected with the Basic Court in Novi Sad decision P2 1435/24, is registered with the Supreme Court as Rev 6599/26. The reply also states that the author’s submission has been forwarded to the reporting judge for information. This establishes a documented response to the earlier inquiry and the administrative action described in the reply. The historical statement that the author had received no acknowledgment on 18 October remains accurate for that earlier publication date; the new reply is recorded separately. The notification does not confirm that the reporting judge has read the submission, visited Origin Dossier or formally incorporated its archive into the case file. It neither decides the appeal nor establishes its outcome. The source cites Article 223(3); this item does not add an interpretation of that provision. The email printout displays 20 October 2026 at 1:59 PM without specifying a time zone. Its internal notification number, 25246-1/26, is distinct from the judicial case number Rev 6599/26.
