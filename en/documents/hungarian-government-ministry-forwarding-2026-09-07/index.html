Hungarian Prime Minister’s Office forwards the identity case to the ministry, with the full bilingual reminder

The Prime Minister’s Office forwards the inquiry to the ministry, with the author copied, under incoming registration number ME_ERK/16706/2026. The four-page record includes his complete English and Hungarian reminder sent on 19 May 2026.

Hungarian Prime Minister’s Office 2026-09-07
The official cover of 7 September 2026, 10:34 AM, is sent by Érkeztetők at the Prime Minister’s Office to TKKM customer service, with office@heriteon.com copied. It states that the forwarding is made on behalf of the Prime Minister’s Cabinet, on grounds of competence, for further handling. Its incoming registration number is ME_ERK/16706/2026. The embedded author email was sent on 19 May 2026 at 8:16 PM to miniszterelnok@mk.gov.hu and erkeztetok@me.gov.hu. In both English and Hungarian, it describes forced assimilation, the identity consequences of two illegal adoptions, the author’s assessment of two levels of Serbian court decisions and SVM’s lack of response as reported then. It also presents the Hungarian composition “Vér emlékezik” and the then-planned Origin Dossier publication. Both complete language versions and the official footer are retained across all four pages. The official forwarding demonstrates administrative handling; the author’s qualifications and requests are not presented as findings adopted by the ministry. The earlier email to peter.magyar@europarl.europa.eu is mentioned but not dated or reproduced as a separate source. Author’s note — 9 October 2026 I regard Hungary with respect: my correspondence was registered and referred to the competent ministry for further handling. This gives me hope that my case will also receive attention within the European Union, of which both my countries of origin, Croatia and Hungary, are members.
