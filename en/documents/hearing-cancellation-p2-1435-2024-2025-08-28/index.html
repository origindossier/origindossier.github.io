Bojana Vujić cancels the hearing scheduled for 3 September 2025: Katarina Stolić’s CSR request and the next date 15 October

The 28 August order cancels the 3 September hearing and sets 15 October at 10:30, 42 days later. The 27 August CSR request cites illness of an unnamed representative and the other representatives’ commitments.

Basic Court in Novi Sad 2025-08-28
The two-page source contains Judge Bojana Vujić’s order of 28 August 2025 in P 2.1435/24 and director Katarina Stolić’s CSR request of 27 August, received by the court that day. The request names illness of an unnamed official representing CSR as defendant and the other authorised representatives’ commitments in other cases. The order cancels 3 September at 10:00 and schedules 15 October at 10:30 in courtroom 210/2, with no further summons. It contains no separate statement of reasons or appeal instruction. Neither supplied page includes a medical certificate. The opening/footer surname difference Vujić/Vulić is preserved in the full source transcription. A separately supplied two-page postal envelope and notice identifies the court sender and case P2.1435/24, Judge Vujić, and bears a visible 2 September 2025 postal stamp. The notice’s attempted-delivery date/time and delivery-signature fields are blank. It does not print 4 September; that personal delivery date comes from the author’s separate account.
