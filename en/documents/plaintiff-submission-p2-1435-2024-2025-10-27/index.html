Plaintiff’s submission of 27 October 2025: complete records, Branislav Čolak’s authority and the continuity of unlawful assimilation

Following the hearing of 15 October, the plaintiff seeks the complete adoption and preceding records, proof of Branislav Čolak’s authority and status, and examination of the original identity evidence. He reaffirms his 2 October requests and separates the law of 1979/1984 from CSR’s conduct in 2025.

Basic Court in Novi Sad 2025-10-27
The six operative pages develop the plaintiff’s case for annulment of the full adoption and restoration of his Croatian and Hungarian identity. He requests the P-1642/76 divorce and O.196/79 probate files, birth and marriage records, the complete 1979 adoption and preceding protective file, and Milorad Vasić’s consent record of 12 September 1984. He links known parental origin, contradictory assessments and the later second-adoption consent to the unlawful identity construct. He asks for Branislav Čolak’s written authority, its exact receipt date and representative status; proposes medical-evidence requirements for future adjournments; and maintains his 2 October requests concerning incapacity and dispatch of the 28 August order. He separates the law applicable to 1979/1984 from the present-day maintenance of the violation, while reserving costs, damages and paternity proceedings without amending the relief sought. The copy is dated 27 October and states registered post with acknowledgment of receipt, but has no court receipt stamp. Nine annex pages and a separate supplementary page reproduce evidence linked to the existing archive.
