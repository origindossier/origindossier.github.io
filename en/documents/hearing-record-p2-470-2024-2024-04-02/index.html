Court procedural record / Primary source

Hearing postponed for lack of procedural conditions — P2.470/24, 2 April 2024

The non-public hearing record names Judge Bojana Vujić. The plaintiff is absent in person but represented by counsel; no one appears for the CSR and service of its summons is not shown. The panel postpones the hearing to 24 April at 11:00.

Basic Court in Novi Sad 2024-04-02
The Basic Court in Novi Sad records proceedings in caseP2.470/24 from 08:15 to 08:30 on 2 April 2024. Judge Bojana Vujić presides. Milan Petrović does not attend in person, but his authorized representative appears; her identity is redacted. No one appears for the defendant CSR. The second page expressly states that the case file does not show service of the defendant’s summons as at the hearing date. Because the procedural conditions for holding the hearing are not met, the panel postpones it to 24 April 2024 at 11:00. Counsel acknowledges the date and undertakes to notify her client; no written summons will be sent to her. The defendant is to be summoned in writing with the submissions dated 1 April 2024.

Article 118 · Reading and correcting the hearing record

Civil Procedure Act of the Republic of Serbia

The form’s printed reference to “particulars under Article 118(1)” does not match the current Act’s numbering. Those particulars are in Article 116(1), to which the current small-claims provision, Article 474, expressly refers. The printed numbering corresponds to Articles 118(1) and 473 of the 2004 Act. This is evidence of an outdated reference in the form; it does not establish that the court applied the former Act or classified this identity/adoption dispute as a small claim.
