Plaintiff’s written submission in P2.1435/24 — 4 December 2024

The plaintiff sets out his written position after the 5 November order. He maintains his claim, requests and evidence, announces that he will notify the court of his chosen lawyer by year-end, and asks that no hearing be scheduled before that notice.

Basic Court in Novi Sad 2024-12-04
The single-page submission is addressed to the Basic Court in Novi Sad in P2.1435/24 and dated 4 December 2024 in Kragujevac. Milan Petrović states that he maintains all the statements in his claim and the requests made against the Centre for Social Work, and emphasizes the proposed and enclosed evidence. He writes that he is acting on the court’s order. The linked 5 November record grants 30 days for his written position and evidence concerning court-fee exemption. This submission also requests that exemption and lists an Agreement on Termination of Employment dated 3 October 2024 as an attachment; the agreement itself is not present in the supplied one-page PDF. He announces that, by the end of the calendar year, he will tell the court which lawyer will represent him, and asks that the next hearing not be scheduled until that notification is received. He asks the court to tell him if any further evidence is needed. The printed date is 29 days after the 5 November hearing. The author states that he submitted the document, while the supplied PDF has no court receipt stamp establishing the receipt date. It records his written requests, not a court decision granting them.

Written response to the 5 November order: claim and evidence maintained; notice of counsel to follow
Why this source is linked
Related source

The event is supported by the written submission dated 4 December 2024 in P2.1435/24. The plaintiff maintains his claim and evidence, says he is responding to the court’s order, and announces notice of his chosen lawyer by year-end while asking that no hearing be scheduled before it. The date is printed in the submission; the PDF does not establish a stamped court receipt date.

I hereby declare that I maintain all the statements made in the claim and the requests I have set out in the proceedings against the defendant Centre for Social Work Novi Sad. I consider the stated grounds of the claim, as well as the proposed and enclosed evidence, to be of crucial importance for establishing the facts and reaching a decision in these proceedings.

Acting on the court’s order, I submit evidence of unemployment, namely the Agreement on Termination of Employment dated 3 October 2024.

Attachment:

  • Agreement on Termination of Employment dated 3 October 2024, a copy enclosed.

This evidence confirms that I have lost my employment, in accordance with the statements in the previous record.

For this reason, and given that my wife and son are unemployed and that I support them, I ask the court to exempt me from paying court fees. Payment of court fees would jeopardize the support of my family and myself, given that we currently have no source of income.

Therefore, I ask the court to grant my request for exemption from court fees, taking into account my current financial circumstances.

I also inform the court that by the end of this calendar year I will notify it which lawyer will represent me in the further course of the proceedings, and I ask the court not to schedule the next hearing until that notification is received.

Please inform me if I need to submit any further evidence.

Kragujevac, 4 December 2024
