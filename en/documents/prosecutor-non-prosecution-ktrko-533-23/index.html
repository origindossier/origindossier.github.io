Prosecutor correspondence / Primary source

Two illegal adoptions reported: prosecutor declines prosecution — KTRKO 533/23 (November 2023)

The author’s two emails of 15 November 2023, the prosecutor’s letter dated 20 November, and the official email transmitting that letter on 21 November. The package preserves what was reported and the written response.

Higher Public Prosecutor's Office in Novi Sad – Special Department for Combating Corruption 2023-11-20
On 15 November 2023 at 10:45 (GMT+1), the author wrote to the anti-corruption department of the Higher Public Prosecutor’s Office in Novi Sad. He described two illegal adoptions, his father’s acknowledgment before a court, removal from the inheritance proceedings, changes of identity, violence and withheld records. He requested information and material concerning a possible investigation of the Social Welfare Centre. At 10:47 he supplied the original Croatian text to correct the machine translation in his first message. The letter dated 20 November 2023, signed by public prosecutor Gordana Rainović, states that criminal prosecution will not be undertaken. Its explanation invokes the inability to establish an offence within that office’s subject-matter and territorial jurisdiction for which prosecution was not time-barred. It does not identify the offences examined, calculate a limitation period, identify documents obtained or checked, or expressly answer whether the reported investigation of the Centre existed. It also states that the office does not collect evidence for parties’ civil litigation. The official covering email is dated 21 November 2023 at 14:35 (GMT+1). This correspondence records the allegations brought to the prosecutor and the response received. The letter contains no finding that the adoptions were lawful and no examination of their nullity. The author’s 2023 statements remain identifiable as his statements. Later clarification by the author: the lawyer from Kragujevac had brought a claim against his sisters concerning inheritance; the author withdrew that claim and ended the lawyer’s engagement because he rejected that approach. His email’s historical description of proceedings to establish paternity is preserved. That withdrawn case is not presented here as the later court proceedings.

Two illegal adoptions and deprivation of identity reported to the prosecutor
Why this source is linked
Source for this event

The correspondence begins with the author’s report on 15 November 2023. This link returns to that event in the chronology.

I am the victim of two illegal adoptions by the Centre for Social Work in Novi Sad. The first was carried out to take away my inheritance after my late father Pavle Lakatoš, who died on 7 November 1978 and with whom I had lived in Bački Vinogradi until then. I was given for illegal adoption on 21 June 1979, only seven days before my fifth birthday, because after that age full adoption was no longer possible under the law then in force. The first illegal adopters were from Budisava. They had their first interview with the social worker on 12 June 1979, with the pedagogue on 18 June 1979 and with the psychologist on 19 June 1979, and the adoption decision was made on 21 June 1979: exactly nine (NINE) days, a record in its own right! I was given for illegal adoption again in September 1984. The law was clearly violated there, because under the law an adopted child cannot be adopted again.

Prosecutor will not undertake criminal prosecution — KTRKO 533/23
Why this source is linked
Source for this event

The prosecutor’s response is dated 20 November 2023. This link returns to that event, with the later email transmission recorded in its details.

We inform you that no criminal prosecution will be undertaken in connection with the submission that you delivered to this Public Prosecutor’s Office on 15 November 2023, because in this specific case it cannot be established that any person committed a criminal offence prosecuted ex officio for which this Public Prosecutor’s Office has subject-matter and territorial jurisdiction and for which the limitation period for criminal prosecution has not expired. I therefore consider that, in this specific matter entered in this Public Prosecutor’s Office’s “KTRKO” register, there are no grounds to initiate criminal proceedings in connection with the described criminal-law event.
