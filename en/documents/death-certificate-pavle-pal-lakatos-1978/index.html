Civil register record / Primary source

Death certificate of Pavle / Pal Lakatoš (1978)

Death record that opens the inheritance and guardianship problem. Pavle’s death is not an isolated life event; it triggers the probate track in which Milivoj Miletić appears as son/heir before being cut out.

Novi Sad Civil Registry Office 1978-11-07
The death certificate is the trigger for the inheritance chain. After Pavle / Pal Lakatoš died on 7 November 1978, the question was not merely who would close an estate file. The question was what would happen to the child whose paternal line had already appeared in court material and who would later enter the probate file as Milivoj Miletić. This document sits directly before the O.196/79 probate contradiction.
