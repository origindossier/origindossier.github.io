First-instance judgment: Bojana Vujić dismisses the annulment claim; disputed reasoning and rejected witnesses

Judgment P2.1435/2024 dated 9 February 2026 rejects the claim for absolute nullity of full adoption. It accepts official documents because their authors were authorized and qualified, accepts the plaintiff’s testimony where it agrees with those documents, and rejects the proposed witnesses. Eight judgment pages and the postal envelope are supplied in full.

Basic Court in Novi Sad 2026-02-09
Bojana Vujić’s panel dismisses the claim for annulment of the 1979 full adoption as absolutely void and decides that each party will bear its own costs. The passage proposing that the claim be upheld is quoted relief; the actual dismissal follows on page 2. The author challenges the judgment’s reasoning and omissions. Page 6 accepts the written documentation because it was issued by authorized and qualified persons; the plaintiff’s testimony is accepted in parts consistent with the evidence already credited. Vinka and Dragan Ignjatić are not heard because the court considers the facts sufficiently examined. Their earlier proposal in the claim and the March 2025 record is distinct from what occurred at the final hearing. The father/son reversal on page 3 is preserved beside the different formulation on page 6. The archived divorce and probate records remain available for direct comparison. Article 30’s six-month condition is quoted on page 7, but the concluding paragraph does not identify the search period or explain the known parental information in the earlier records. Article 40 is mentioned in the reproduction of the 1979 record; the 1984 adoption is recorded separately without resolving that relationship in the reasoning. Those specific passages are linked to the author’s argument concerning nullity and forced assimilation. The judgment is dated 9 February 2026. The envelope bears a 12 March 2026 postal stamp; the subsequent appeal records receipt on 13 March. The supplied package comprises eight judgment pages, the envelope and its enlarged detail. The appeal notice gives 15 days from service, not from the date printed on the judgment.
