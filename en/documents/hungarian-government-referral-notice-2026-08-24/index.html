Hungarian Prime Minister’s Office: notice of referral to the competent ministry

The Prime Minister’s Office informs Milan Lakatoš Petrović that his inquiry is being referred to the Ministry Responsible for Social Relations and Culture for further handling, under file registration number ID_ME-IF/332/2/2026.

Hungarian Prime Minister’s Office 2026-08-24
The email dated 24 August 2026 at 12:54 PM is sent by Érkeztetők, erkeztetok@me.gov.hu, to office@heriteon.com and signed Miniszterelnökség. It records an administrative response to the inquiry and identifies the competent ministry for further handling. Iktatószám identifies the file registration number ID_ME-IF/332/2/2026. The cited Government Decree 90/2026 (V.13.) is the stated basis for the competence referral. The source does not contain an assessment of the adoptions, identity evidence or Serbian judgments, or a substantive reply from the receiving ministry. The September forwarding is a separate dated record in the same supplied correspondence. Author’s note — 9 October 2026 I regard Hungary with respect: my correspondence was registered and referred to the competent ministry for further handling. This gives me hope that my case will also receive attention within the European Union, of which both my countries of origin, Croatia and Hungary, are members.
