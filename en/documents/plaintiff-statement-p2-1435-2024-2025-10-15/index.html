Case submission / Primary source

Written statement of 15 October 2025: forced assimilation, void adoption and the plaintiff’s own account

The plaintiff’s two-page statement sets out his qualifications of the 1979 and 1984 acts and their lifelong consequences. The hearing record confirms that Bojana Vujić received it and that a copy was given to the CSR representative.

Basic Court in Novi Sad 2025-10-15
The statement is a distinct source accompanying the plaintiff’s examination on 15 October. It preserves his account of the loss of Croatian and Hungarian origins, language and identity, the absence of maternal consent, known paternity, the age threshold and the second-adoption paradox. His terms forced assimilation, void adoption and legal forgery remain intact. The concluding names describe the same person through successive entries, not different children. The hearing record confirms that the text was placed in the file and a copy handed to the defendant’s representative. That procedural receipt is distinct from judicial acceptance of its legal conclusions.
