Order / application for entry in the civil register of births

Primary civil-register source for the original birth identity: Milivoj Miletić, born in Novi Sad on 28 June 1974, with Cvita Miletić recorded as mother and Croatian recorded as the mother’s narodnost. This is the starting point before the Vasić adoptive overwrite.

Novi Sad Civil Registry Office 1974-06-28
This document is the first identity anchor. It records the child before the adoption machinery: Milivoj Miletić, born in Novi Sad on 28 June 1974, with Cvita Miletić in the maternal line. For the dossier it is not background. It is the baseline against which every later administrative rewrite is measured: Milan Vasić in 1979 and Petrović Milan in 1984 are later imposed states, not the original birth identity.
