Submission of 2 October 2025: objection to late notice, evidence of absence and court dispatch records

The plaintiff records delivery on 4 September, after the canceled hearing, and requests evidence for the CSR representative’s absence and the court registry’s dispatch record. The six-page PDF includes the envelope, CSR request and cancellation order.

Basic Court in Novi Sad 2025-10-02
The first two pages are the plaintiff’s submission dated 2 October 2025; the remaining four are its documentary annexes. It sets out the sequence of the 28 August order, 2 September postal stamp, arrival at court on 3 September and delivery on 4 September. It requests a medical certificate and issuance records, preservation of the original, consequences if justification is not supplied, and the date and time of dispatch from the court. It also asks that the examination proceed on 15 October. These are specific requests to the court, not orders already made by Bojana Vujić. The 27 October submission expressly maintains them. The written date is established; proof of the court’s receipt is not included.

Related source

The 2 October submission challenges the notification and justification of the September cancellation and attaches the envelope, CSR request and order. The order remains a separate source; the plaintiff’s requests are not presented as its operative terms.

  • The hearing for my examination was scheduled for 3 September 2025 at 10:00.
  • That morning I saw no notice for this case on the court’s noticeboard; after enquiring at the court, I was informed that the hearing had been postponed because of “health reasons affecting the defendant’s representative”.
  • The court’s order/notice is dated 28 August 2025, bears a Novi Sad postal postmark of 2 September 2025, and was delivered to me on 4 September 2025 (a photocopy of the envelope is attached).
  • The defendant’s request of 27 August 2025 (no. 560-111-1/2024) refers to the representative’s illness, but is not accompanied by any medical documentation.

This sequence — postal postmark 2 September → delivery 4 September, after the hearing date of 3 September — objectively calls into question both the timeliness of service and the justification for the postponement.
