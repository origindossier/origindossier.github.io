Author’s account: arrival on 5 June 2025, oral explanation and acceptance of 3 September

The author says he attended at the scheduled time on 5 June. The judge told him orally that the CSR lawyer had broken her leg; he was shown no evidence of the reason, accepted 3 September and received no written document about the change.

2025-06-05
The 26 March court record scheduled 5 June 2025 at 10:00. The author states that he attended at that time and heard the judge explain orally that the CSR lawyer “had broken her leg”. He says no evidence of this reason was shown to him. He accepted 3 September 2025 as the next hearing date and received no written document concerning the change. This separately supplied account is preserved as the author’s testimony. It is connected to the earlier written scheduling record; no new court record or PDF was supplied.
