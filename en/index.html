Origin Dossier: A Documented Case of State-Enabled Identity Substitution and Forced Assimilation

A Hungarian-Croatian child was absorbed into a replacement identity through an unlawful adoption process whose legal and institutional consequences continue into the present.

This archive confronts the reader with a state-enabled erasure of identity: a child of Hungarian-Croatian origin subjected to forced assimilation, a living mother bypassed, and an unlawful substitution of biological continuity whose consequences are still protected through the legal and institutional continuity of the Republic of Serbia.

The site is structured as a public dossier: contradictions first, then timeline, documents, evidence, and legal analysis.

12 December 1979 · Probate proceedings

Appeal attacks Milivoj’s heir status

The appeal turns Milivoj’s already visible heirship into a contested status and starts the procedural route toward his exclusion.

Full record text
The appeal is the procedural opening through which Milivoj’s recognized inheritance position becomes attackable. The child had already appeared in the probate logic of Pavle / Pal Lakatoš; the appeal then turns that visible heirship into a disputed status. The decisive issue is not only that an appeal was filed. The adoption route enters the probate dispute as an argument against the child’s inheritance position. That raises the hard question: how did the appellant’s counsel obtain and use adoption information in an inheritance file if full-adoption files and data are official secret under Article 39 of the 1976 SAPV Adoption Act? Read with Articles 35, 37, 38, 39 and 41. Article 35 concerns the new birth registration after full adoption; Article 37 cuts the legal effects toward blood relatives; Article 38 blocks establishment of paternity and maternity after full adoption; Article 39 makes the full-adoption file and annulled birth entry an official secret; Article 41 governs nullity where the full-adoption conditions were not met. This is not a clean appellate correction. It is the point where a secret adoption-administrative track is used inside probate to destabilize the child’s already recorded inheritance position.

Author’s note

I am convinced that the competent authorities followed my public appearances. Through television broadcasting, I addressed a much wider audience than the view counts of later uploads to YouTube show.

I had to investigate my own identity, obtain evidence, bring court proceedings and bear their burden myself. This is work that a serious state ought to carry out through its institutions. In the case of the Republic of Serbia, I use the expression “serious state” with explicit reservations.

Despite my public appearances and the evidence submitted, the burden of initiating and pursuing the proceedings remained on me. In good faith, I offered a settlement to resolve the matter peacefully and restore my original identity and origin. Nevertheless, my claim was rejected and my appeal dismissed.
