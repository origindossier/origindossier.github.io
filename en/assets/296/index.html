Editorial context - not part of the portal record

The source preserves an older court heading and a Gzp selection in the search form. The actual case result is REV 6599/2026. The translation does not turn the form selection into a different classification of the displayed case.

The receipt and assignment of 13 July 2026, the document entry of 19 October 2026, and the supply of this PDF to the archive on 20 October 2026 are separate dates. No capture timestamp is invented. The entry “Submission dated 19 October 2026” is not evidence, by itself, of another submission newly sent by the author that day.

The Court Registry’s reply of 20 October 2026 expressly refers to the inquiry of 18 October, confirms registration as Rev 6599/26 and states that the author’s submission was forwarded to the reporting judge for information. The underlying appeal on points of law remains a separate document. Neither source establishes that the judge has read the submission, visited the archive, formally added its contents to the case file, or decided the merits.
