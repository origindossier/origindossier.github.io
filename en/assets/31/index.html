Municipal Court in Subotica
Case No.: O. 2470/78
Date: 10 January 1979
Page marker on scan: 4/3

A record drawn up before the Municipal Court in Subotica on 10 January 1979, in the probate matter of the late Pavle Lakatoš.

Persons present on behalf of the court

Judge: Marko Krnajski
Court clerk: Erika Šipoš

Before opening the probate hearing, the judge warned the parties that they have the right to an interpreter for the Hungarian language and that they have the right to use their own language.

Upon the court’s summons, Verona Lakatoš, the divorced spouse of the deceased, appeared.

After the reading of death certificate no. 31 of 10 November 1978, the appearing party states that the other heirs are prevented from attending, and that Roža Koplanj did not receive the summons; it was returned with the notation that the address was insufficient.

The appearing party states that Roža Koplanj lives in Novi Sad, at Slana Bara, Zadružna 25.

The appearing party states that, during the lifetime of the deceased, litigation had been initiated concerning the division of the deceased’s property and the determination of her ownership share on the basis of marital/community property, and that the proceedings were discontinued after the death of the deceased; and that the real estate, that is, all property, constitutes joint marital/community property because it was acquired during the marriage, and she requests that one half be recognized as belonging to her in ownership, while the other half is the subject matter of the estate.

The court issues the following

DECISION

Today’s hearing is adjourned to 15 February 1979 at 10:00 a.m. The appearing party is notified of this by proclamation of this decision and is ordered to obtain accurate information regarding the property of the deceased. A written summons shall be sent to Roža Kopljan of Novi Sad, Slana Bara, Zadružna 25, and to Iren Lakatoš and Ištvan Lakatoš, at Novi Sad, Trg Majke Jevrosime no. 5, with the indication that Verona Lakatoš is asserting a claim regarding the joint marital/community property of the deceased.

Judge: [signature]
Court clerk: [signature]
Party: Lakatoš Verona [handwritten signature]

I, Róža Kopljan, accept the inheritance as belongs to me by law.
I, Vera Lakatoš, on behalf of my minor son Istvan Lakatoš, accept the inheritance as belongs to us by law.

Attorney Helena Uzelac states that, besides the reported heirs, there is also the minor Milivoj Miletić, aged 5, for whom it has not been judicially established that the deceased was his father, but the deceased did acknowledge him, and the minor Milivoj is currently with his grandmother and grandfather, that is, with the parents of the deceased. Therefore, in accordance with that, it should be obtained from the Centre for Social Work that a guardian be appointed for the protection of his interests.

The Court issues the following

DECISION

Today's hearing is concluded. The next one will be scheduled in writing.

Date: 21 August 1979
Novi Sad

The Self-Managing Community of Interest for Social Protection, Novi Sad, on the basis of Article 1 of the Decision entrusting guardianship authority tasks to the Self-Managing Community of Interest for Social Protection, Novi Sad (Official Gazette of the Municipality of Novi Sad, No. 8/77), and Article 202 of the Law on General Administrative Procedure (Official Gazette of the SFRY, Nos. 18/65 and 4/77), deciding upon the request of the Municipal Court in Novi Sad, File No. O.196/79, hereby issues the following

DECISION

I. Miloš Lisica, attorney from Novi Sad, M. Gorkog No. 6, is hereby appointed temporary guardian for the minor Milivoj Miletić for the protection of his rights and interests in the proceedings before the Municipal Court in Novi Sad, File No. O.196/79.

II. The temporary guardian is obliged, after the completion of these proceedings, to inform the authority about their outcome.

Reasoning

On 30 July 1979, the Municipal Court in Novi Sad addressed this authority with a request that a temporary guardian be appointed for the minor Milivoj Miletić in order to protect his rights and interests before the Municipal Court in Novi Sad in the probate matter concerning the estate of the late Pavle Lakatoš.

Upon considering this request, this authority found it well-founded and therefore decided as stated in the operative part of this decision, appointing attorney Miloš Lisica as temporary guardian, considering him, as a professional person, to be the most suitable person to perform the duties of guardian in these proceedings.

An appeal against this decision may be filed within 15 days from the date of receipt thereof to the Provincial Secretariat for Health and Social Policy of SAPV, directly or by post. The appeal shall be submitted in writing or orally on the record, and it is exempt from fees.

  3. Archives
