The hearing begins at 9:00. The hearing is not public.

The presiding judge opens the main hearing, announces the subject of the hearing and establishes who is present.

For the plaintiff: in person; Lukač Agošton, attorney from Temerin.

Counsel for the plaintiff withdraws the claim concerning Lakatoš Irena, as she has reached adulthood and will pursue her rights to maintenance in separate proceedings. Counsel reduces the claim concerning Lakatoš Ištvan and requests 600 dinars per month, calculated from the date the action was brought and for as long as the statutory conditions exist.

The defendant does not oppose the claim as formulated. As regards fault, he asks the court to establish the fault of both parties, since the plaintiff is also at fault for the divorce.

Regarding evidence that the plaintiff is at fault for the divorce, he requests a period of 15 days in which to provide evidence to the court.

The court issues the following ORDER.

The witnesses shall be heard.

Witness MOJSINOV SPASA

Daughter of Jakšić Damjan, born in 1916 in Mol, from Novi Sad, Teodora Pavlovića Street no. 34, unrelated to the parties, having been warned, states:

I have known the parties for 12 years. I know that they lived badly together. Four years ago, the defendant began living with Miletić Cveta and had a child with her. I heard that he also lived with a woman called Julka and that she had an abortion; that is what Julka told me.

The defendant told me that he had quarreled with the plaintiff because she had not paid him for the milk he brought from the farmstead. Since then he had completely separated from the family. As far as I know, the plaintiff treated the defendant well as a wife. I know that she gave him money, helped with work on the farmstead and called him home.

She lent him the money.

The witness does not claim expenses.

Counsel for the plaintiff maintains the lawsuit and the claim.

Counsel for the defendant maintains the previous allegations of the defense and the counterclaim.

Both seek the exclusive fault of the opposing party.

Counsel for the plaintiff explains the request that the court dissolve the marriage through fault, taking the view that the fact that the defendant fathered an extramarital child during the marriage points to his culpable conduct, because this led to the disturbance of relations in the marriage. It is particularly emphasized that a finding of fault is necessary for further proceedings concerning the determination of the holder of occupancy rights, in which fault plays a significant role.

Counsel for the defendant also insists on the plaintiff's fault. He states that she had rejected him sexually since 1968, when she converted to a new religion which forbids sexual relations between a man and a woman. He states that in 1971 she threw him out of the house, so that the defendant had to live on the farmstead, which he had built himself. Completely separated, a heart patient, left to himself, he truly entered into life with another woman, with whom he fathered a child.

Because of illness, an orchard was also purchased in Bački Vinogradi and leased out, because he cannot cultivate it himself. He returned to Novi Sad and lives in the stable of the parties' house even though one room in the house is completely empty. The parties' children know this situation; even the daughter of the parties is raising the defendant's child, because she understands the situation and the plaintiff's fault in her relations toward him. He proposes the examination of the parties' children as witnesses, namely KOPLJAN ROŽIKA, Zadruga 25, Novi Sad. Because of the illness of the witness, he withdraws the request to examine Vukadinović Magdalena.

The court issues the following

ORDER

The witness Vukadinović Magdalena will not be heard.

Counsel for the plaintiff disputes the allegations of the defense insofar as they are contrary to the plaintiff's allegations. Above all, he emphasizes that the breakdown of marital relations occurred in 1975 and not in 1971, and that it occurred because of the defendant's extramarital child. The reason is therefore moral and not material in nature. He claims that the defendant lives in Bački Vinogradi in a six-room house, part of which he let to his sister free of charge. He only comes to Novi Sad, spends the night in the stable, especially because the house is small and does not satisfy the needs either of the plaintiff or of her children with whom she lives in the house.

It is undisputed between the parties that the defendant will pay maintenance for the minor Ištvan in the amount of 700 dinars per month. It is also undisputed that the plaintiff receives a net monthly amount of 860 dinars in rent for the garages and the laundry room, that she pays tax on that amount in the sum of 940 dinars annually, or 78 dinars monthly, so that the net income on this basis is 782 dinars, or 390 dinars monthly for each party. The District Court in Novi Sad, sitting in a panel composed of Judge Nikola Stepić as presiding judge, and lay judges Popović Sofija and Petrović Lazar as members of the panel, in the divorce matter of the plaintiff Lakatoš Vera, from Novi Sad, Trg Majke Jevrosime no. 3, represented by Lukač Agošton, attorney from Temerin, against the defendant Lakatoš Pavle, from Novi Sad, Trg Majke Jevrosime no. 3, represented by Helena Uzelac, attorney from Novi Sad, concerning divorce, the value of the dispute for court-fee purposes being 2,000 dinars, on 26 October 1977, rendered, without pronouncing publicly, the following:

JUDGMENT

I – The marriage concluded before the registrar of the People's Committee of the City of Senta on 17 November 1951, entered in the marriage register under no. 231/1951, between Lakatoš Pavle, son of Stjepan, born on 25 January 1928 in Hrtkovci, Municipality of Ruma, and Lakatoš Vera-Terezija, née Guljaš, daughter of Guljaš Stevan, born in Senta on 22 February 1931, is DISSOLVED BY THE COURT on the basis of Articles 60 and 62 of the SAP Vojvodina Marriage Act, through the mutual fault of the parties.

II – The minor child of the parties, Lakatoš Ištvan, born in Novi Sad on 3 August 1962, is AWARDED BY THE COURT for custody, upbringing, and maintenance to the mother, Lakatoš Vera-Terezija, née Guljaš.

III – The defendant, Lakatoš Pavle, the father, is obliged to pay, as his share of the contribution for the maintenance of his son Ištvan, 310 dinars per month, beginning on 16 November 1976, as the date of filing of the action, and continuing so long as the legal conditions exist, in advance by every tenth day of the month, into the hands of the child's mother Lakatoš Vera-Terezija, née Guljaš; any accrued installments are to be paid in a lump sum within 15 days, all under threat of enforcement.

IV – Each party bears its own costs.

Reasoning

In the lawsuit of 16 November 1976, the plaintiff stated that in the long-standing marriage there had been alienation between the spouses, especially because they had separated economically and spatially, with the defendant cultivating the land and the plaintiff conducting the business of the grain mill; that the defendant had begun living with another woman, with whom he had fathered a child; and she therefore sought that the court dissolve the marriage through the exclusive fault of the defendant. As regards maintenance of the child—the minor Ištvan—she specified her claim at the main-hearing session of 6 October 1977. In the lawsuit she also requested that the court order the defendant to pay the litigation costs.

The defendant did not oppose the divorce, but sought that the court find mutual fault, because the plaintiff was also to blame for the breakdown of the marriage. He agreed regarding the maintenance of the minor Ištvan after the clarified claim of 6 October 1977.

Reconciliation of the spouses was unsuccessful, because the parties are categorical in demanding that the marriage be dissolved, especially since they are separated and the defendant has an extramarital child.

The court conducted the evidentiary proceedings by reading the extract from the marriage register of the People's Committee of the City of Senta no. 231/1951; the extract from the birth register of the Municipality of Novi Sad no. 1595/62 of 3 August 1962; the certificate of the Primary School “Svetozar Marković-Toza,” Novi Sad, of 3 November 1976; the certificate of the Novi Sad Municipal Assembly no. 05-437/2-1317 of 23 December 1975; by hearing the parties; by hearing the witnesses Mojsinov Spasa, Dimić Eržebet, Miletić Cvita, Mijatović Đorđe, Fekete Franja, Bicok Margita; by taking requested testimony from Gabrić Etelka and Gabrić Nikola; and by hearing witness Kopljan Roža; and on the basis of this evidentiary proceedings and the facts undisputed between the parties, it established the following FACTUAL STATE:

The parties concluded marriage before the registrar of the People's Committee of the City of Senta on 17 November 1951. Three children were born in the marriage, two now of full age; the minor Lakatoš Ištvan was born on 3 August 1962.

From the very beginning of the marriage, disagreements arose because of the different natures of the parties. The plaintiff is wilful, inclined to giving orders, a stronger personality, sexually uninterested, and did not tolerate the defendant's occasional drunkenness—which at first was even amusing to her—but later developed into alcoholism. The defendant was treated for alcoholism, without success, and all this led to quarrels and the separation of the spouses, so that the defendant increasingly withdrew into himself and cheated on the plaintiff with other women, which she did not tolerate. She insulted him, calling him a peasant (pejoratively), physically attacked him, and broke his nose. Such relations also led to economic separation. Go...

They completely separated their assets and behaved toward one another as though they were strangers (the plaintiff sold bran to the defendant, sold him meat, etc.). The defendant came home very rarely; in the final years the plaintiff did not cook for him, nor wash for him, so he organized all of this on the land, building a hut of wattle and mud, unsuitable for winter habitation. In the view of this court, in the present case, the conditions under Articles 60 and 62 of the SAP Vojvodina Marriage Act were met. The defendant is responsible because he drank, because he cheated on the plaintiff, because he began living with another woman and fathered a son with her. On the other hand, the plaintiff is also responsible for the divorce because she is wilful, insulted the defendant, beat him, did not care for him when he moved to live on the land, allowing him to live in a hut built of wattle and mud and now to sleep in the stable of the house, and because she rejected him sexually, thus she herself also contri-
