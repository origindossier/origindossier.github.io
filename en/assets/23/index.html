The Higher Court in Novi Sad, composed of Judge Eva Popović,
as presiding judge of the panel, and Lazar Petrović and Milan Vidović,
as panel members, in the legal matter of VASIĆ MILORAD from Sremski
Karlovci, Sl. Rodića 8, represented by Dušan Uzelac, attorney
from Futog, against the defendant VASIĆ GOSPAVA from Budisava, Petefi
Šandora 172, represented by authorized representative Dragan Prodanović,
LL.B. - adviser in the Bureau for Providing Legal Aid in Novi Sad,
in a divorce action, and regarding the motion for issuance of an
interim measure dated 3.IX.1982, hereby renders the following

INTERIM MEASURE

The plaintiff Vasić Milorad is ORDERED, until the final
conclusion of these proceedings, to pay for child support
for the minor Milan the amount of 2,000.00 dinars, and for spousal
support to Vasić Gospava the amount of 1,500.00 dinars,
starting from 3.IX.1982, under threat of enforcement.

An appeal against this interim measure does not stay
enforcement.

Reasoning

By submission dated 3.IX.1982, the defendant - counter-
plaintiff filed a motion requesting that the court issue an interim
measure, whereby the plaintiff would be obliged, until the final
conclusion of these proceedings, to pay a contribution for the support
of the minor Milan, as well as an amount for spousal support to be
determined by the court within the limits of the claim asserted,
starting from 1.IX.1982.

The attorney of the plaintiff - counter-defendant, regarding
the request of the defendant - counter-plaintiff for issuance of an
interim measure, both for child and spousal support, left the matter
entirely to the court's assessment. He emphasized that, in his view,
for determining spousal support by interim measure it would be
necessary for the court to have information on the working capacity
of the defendant - counter-plaintiff.

In the evidentiary proceedings, the court conducted evidence
by inspection and reading of the extract from the marriage register of the Municipality
of Novi Sad - Petrovaradin branch office, recorded under current no. 468 for 1962,
and the extract from the birth register of the Municipality of Novi Sad

file of the Municipal Court in Novi Sad, ref. no. P.3017/82,
the medical certificate for the defendant - counter-plaintiff from the said
file, no. 3/82 dated 27.IV.1982, issued by the "Health Center"
Novi Sad, OOUR General Medicine, Budisava Dispensary, the report
with findings and opinion of the attending physician of the same health
station in Budisava no. 213 dated 3.IX.1982, the report on the
earnings of the plaintiff - counter-defendant "Novograp" OOUR
"Craft Operations" Novi Sad, no. 02-17/258 dated 9.IX.1982,
and after hearing the plaintiff - counter-defendant and the defendant - counter-
plaintiff as parties, it established the following facts upon evaluation:

The parties entered into marriage on 21.I.1962 in
Petrovaradin. The marriage of the parties had been more than harmonious for a full
18 years. They had no children in the marriage. Therefore, on 21.VI.1979
they adopted the minor Milan. From that time disagreements arose
between the parties. First because of the child, his upbringing,
and later of a personal nature. Over time the relations between the parties
deteriorated to such an extent that the plaintiff finally left the marital
union at the beginning of March 1982. The defendant remained in the house
with the child, while the plaintiff lives as a subtenant. From his employment
the plaintiff earns an average monthly personal income in the
amount of 13,760.92 dinars. On 18 January 1982, the defendant had
her left breast completely removed by surgery, and is therefore
unfit for work.

These facts were established by the court from the evidence
listed above and presented.

Assessing the evidence presented, the court formed the conviction
that the disturbance of marital relations occurred exclusively after
the adoption of the child and therefore found that neither party bears
fault for the disturbance of relations in the marriage.

Based on the facts thus established,
the court found that there are conditions for issuing an interim measure
both for child support, considering that the plaintiff does not pay it,
and for spousal support. This is for the reason that the plaintiff
has not paid support for the minor Milan since the month of May of this year,
although by law he is obliged to do so. The court finds that there are conditions
for issuing an interim measure also for spousal support, because
the defendant is unfit for work and has no means of subsistence, and since
she is not at fault for the disturbance and breakdown of relations in the marriage
and marital union, in the opinion of this court she is entitled to spousal
support, considering that in the opinion of this court the conditions
from Article 83, paragraphs 1 and 2 of the SAPV Marriage Act are fulfilled. Therefore,
the plaintiff, who is able to do so, was ordered
to pay the amounts determined by this interim measure both to the minor Milan
and to the defendant.

Based on Article 83, paragraph 3 of the SAPV Marriage Act,
the court decided that an appeal against this decision does not stay the
execution of the decision - the interim measure.

Reasoning

By the complaint dated 23.VII.1982, the plaintiff
requested that the court dissolve his marriage with the defendant by application
of Article 60 of the SAPV Marriage Act, with each party bearing
its own costs.

On 20.VIII.1982, the defendant - counter-plaintiff
filed a counterclaim with the court requesting that her marriage with
the plaintiff - counter-defendant be dissolved due to his exclusive fault,
that the child be entrusted to her, and that the plaintiff - counter-defendant
be ordered to pay, for the support of the minor Milan, 3,500.00 dinars per month,
and that the court also order him to pay her, in the name of spousal support,
3,500.00 dinars per month from the day of filing
the counterclaim and for as long as the legal conditions exist, as well as
that the court order him to reimburse her for all incurred litigation
costs.

Pursuant to Article 77 of the SAPV Marriage Act,
the court attempted reconciliation of the spouses, which remained unsuccessful.
This for the reason that the plaintiff stated that, for him,
relations in the marriage had become disturbed to the point of unbearable intensity, while the defendant
stated that she wished to reconcile, finding that for her the relations in
the marriage were not disturbed to such an extent that the marriage had to be dissolved.

By submission dated 3.IX.1982, the defendant - counter-
plaintiff proposed that the court issue an interim measure,
whereby it would oblige the plaintiff, until the final conclusion
of these proceedings, to pay by way of contribution to the support of the minor
Milan, as well as by way of spousal support, an amount determined by the court
within the limits of the claim asserted, starting
from 1.IX.1982.

established the following FACTUAL STATE:

The parties concluded marriage on 21.I.1962
in Petrovaradin. The marriage of the parties was more than harmonious for a full
18 years. They had no children in the marriage, and on 21.VI.1979
they adopted the minor Milan. From that time disagreements arose
between the parties. At first concerning the child and his upbringing,
and later of a personal nature. Over time the relations between the parties
were disturbed to such an extent that the plaintiff - counter-defendant
left the marital union at the beginning of March 1982 and since then it has not
been re-established. In the parties' house remained the defendant - counter-plaintiff
with the child, and she uses the house together with a garden of 14 ares. During this spring
and summer the defendant - counter-plaintiff cultivated the garden. The plaintiff -
counter-defendant in the period from 1.IX.1981 to 31.VII.1982
earned an average monthly personal income in the amount of 13,760.92
dinars. The plaintiff - counter-defendant lives in Sremski Karlovci
as a subtenant, and for rent pays 4,000.00 dinars,
in addition to which he also pays for water and electricity. The defendant - counter-plaintiff
underwent surgery on 18 January 1982, and on that occasion
her left breast was completely removed, and she is not fit for work.

These facts were established by the court from the evidence
listed above.

Based on the thus established factual situation,
the court found that the basic cause of the disturbance in the relations of the parties
in marriage, which occurred after the adoption of the minor Milan,
was the difference in their understanding and manner of raising and educating
the child. This is indicated by their conduct and manifestations during their
life after the adoption of the child, regardless of the fact that there were not
more serious conflicts between them. This indicates that the parties do not have a unified
view on the manner of raising and educating the child. Therefore,
also on the further manner and purpose of living. Accordingly, the court finds that
all conditions for application of Article 60 of the SAPV Marriage Act were met, and
therefore dissolved the marriage of the parties on that basis.

In deciding on the placement of the minor Milan, the court
accepted the agreement of the parties that he be entrusted to the mother, finding that
he is still of such an age that a mother's care and love
are necessary to him.

In deciding on the amount of the plaintiff-counter-
defendant's contribution to the support of the minor Milan, the court took into account
the plaintiff's financial circumstances and abilities, and the needs of the minor Milan
having regard to his age, and found that for his support
at least about 2,500.00 dinars are necessary. The court finds that the plaintiff
is able to pay this amount having regard to the income he
earns. In doing so, the court also took into account that the plaintiff
is a subtenant and pays 4,000.00 dinars in rent,
as well as that he also has costs for electricity and water. Nevertheless,
the court considers that by setting aside the amount for the minor Milan,
his personal subsistence will not be endangered, bearing in mind his
other expenses. As for the defendant, as the mother, the court finds
that she, considering that she herself is also a dependent person, could contribute

As regards the claim of the defendant - counter-plaintiff
for spousal support, the court, when deciding, took into account
that the defendant - counter-plaintiff has no means of support and
is unfit for work, and since she bears no fault for the divorce,
the court considers that the conditions from Article 63 of the SAPV
Marriage Act are fulfilled. Therefore, the plaintiff - counter-defendant
was ordered to pay the defendant -
counter-plaintiff, in the name of spousal support, 2,000.00 dinars
per month, finding that this amount is appropriate to the means
of the plaintiff - counter-defendant, and that the defendant - counter-plaintiff
will be able to cover her basic needs with this amount. In doing so, the court
also took into account that the defendant lives in the parties' house and
does not pay rent, as well as that she has a garden from which she can supplement
the needs of the household, both for herself and for the child.

The court rejected the excess of the claim for the support of the minor Milan
above the awarded amount of 2,500.00 dinars and up to 3,500.00 dinars,
finding that the claim had been set too high, both with regard
to the plaintiff's means and to the needs of the child. The court
also rejected the excess of the claim for support above
the awarded amount of 2,000.00 dinars and up to 3,500.00 dinars. This
for the reason that, in the opinion of this court, by awarding a higher amount
for spousal support, the subsistence of the plaintiff
would be jeopardized, because his obligation determined by this decision for the support
of the child and the defendant amounts to 4,500.00 dinars; when to this
there is added the amount of the rent of 4,000.00 dinars, the plaintiff
is left with about 5,439.00 dinars for living, not counting the costs of electricity
and water.

The decision on litigation costs is based on Article
87, paragraph 1 of the SAPV Marriage Act.
