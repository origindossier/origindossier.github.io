ORDER

The proceedings are resumed by reading the case file. The case file is read.

Separate author’s account — disputed reading of the case file

The author expressly disputes both assertions that the file was read: the initial statement that the proceedings were resumed by reading the file and the final statement that all written documentation was read individually and by name. He states that nothing was read at this hearing. They only concluded that everything had been said and that he maintained everything he had presented orally and in writing.

Editorial source connection. The entry about revoked authority does not establish that self-representation began on 9 February 2026: the 5 November 2024 record and 6 February 2025 record already document the earlier representation history. This final record names no witnesses and contains no decision rejecting Vinka or Dragan Ignjatić. Their examination was proposed in the earlier statement of claim and maintained in the 26 March 2025 record. Those earlier proposals must be distinguished from the author’s account of what he raised at the final hearing and from the general withdrawal formulation recorded here.


                        


                            

        Comments on source passages (10)
            

                
ZPP — Article 118 · Reading and correcting the hearing record

                
The form’s printed reference to “particulars under Article 118(1)” does not match the current Act’s numbering. Those particulars are in Article 116(1), to which the current small-claims provision, Article 474, expressly refers. The printed numbering corresponds to Articles 118(1) and 473 of the 2004 Act. This is evidence of an outdated reference in the form; it does not establish that the court applied the former Act or classified this identity/adoption dispute as a small claim.
