16 April 1979
Novi Sad

The Self-Managing Interest Community of Social Protection Novi Sad, deciding in the matter of the placement of the minor Miletić Milivoj, competent within the meaning of Article 87 of the Statute of the Self-Managing Interest Community of Social Protection Novi Sad and Articles 7 and 52 of the Decision on Social Protection of Citizens in the Territory of the Municipality of Novi Sad, issues the following:

DECISION

The minor Miletić Milivoj, born on 28 June 1974 in Novi Sad, shall be placed with the family of Vizmeg Julka, residing in Irig, on 2 April 1979.

The amount of 1,200.00 dinars (one thousand two hundred dinars), which is the monthly maintenance for the child in the family, shall be borne by the Self-Managing Interest Community of Social Protection Novi Sad.

Payment of the above amount shall begin as of 2 April 1979.

Reasoning

The professional team of this Community considered the issue of the care of the minor Miletić Milivoj and proposed that he be placed with a foster family because he had remained without parental care.

Considering the proposal of the professional team and the available documents, it was established that the mother of the minor Miletić Milivoj was at an unknown place of residence and that his father was unknown, and that he had remained without parental care.

From the above stated, and within the meaning of Article 24 of the Decision on Social Protection of Citizens in the Territory of the Municipality of Novi Sad, it was necessary to decide as stated in the operative part.

A dissatisfied party has the right to appeal this decision to the Commission of the Self-Managing Interest Community of Social Protection Novi Sad for deciding on appeals against decisions on rights from basic social protection, within 15 days of receipt thereof, through this Community.

  1. Miletić Cvita,
  2. Vizmeg Julka, Irig
  3. SIZ of social protection,
  4. Department for Social Services and General Administration of the Municipal Assembly of Irig,
  5. Archive.

                        Broader comment
                        
This passage belongs to confidential social-welfare dossier 3871/79. Vera Radovanović’s own operative file does not contain an empty paternal field in substance; it contains a suppressed paternal trail. On 5 April she writes that the child’s natural father had died. On 8 April she writes that Pal Lakatoš raised the child and presented himself as the child’s natural father. In the same page she again uses the formula after the death of the natural father. On 16 April she places the child with Vizmeg Julka, the sister of father Pal, whom the child knew. This is not neutral uncertainty. The file treats Pal as father when that serves placement and care, then erases him when the legal identity channel is filled in. The mother is attacked through neighbor-based moral claims while the child is recorded as health insured through her, healthy, neat, well cared for, and later well nourished. The evidentiary cut is clear: Vera had the mother trace, the Pal/father trace, the paternal-family placement trace and the child’s Pal-name reaction in the same file, yet those traces were not converted into protection of identity, paternity and inheritance position before adoption.
