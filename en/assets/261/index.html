Article 205

Investigative principle

In proceedings concerning family relationships, the court may establish facts even when they are not disputed between the parties, and may independently investigate facts that neither party has presented.

Article 207

Costs of proceedings

The court decides on reimbursement of costs in proceedings concerning family relationships at its discretion, taking considerations of equity into account.

Article 208

Revision

Revision is always admissible in proceedings concerning family relationships, unless this Act provides otherwise.

Article 274

Territorial jurisdiction in adoption annulment

In proceedings for the annulment of adoption, territorial jurisdiction lies with the court in whose territory the guardianship authority before which the adoption was established is located.

Article 358

Adoptions established before this Act

(1) Adoption established before this Act began to apply is governed by the regulations that were in force up to the date on which this Act began to apply.

(2) Adoption established under the provisions of this Act is considered full adoption for the purposes of other laws.
