Supreme Court confirmation of registration as Rev 6599/26 and forwarding of the submission to the reporting judge - 20 October 2026

Notification, internal number 25246-1/26

Full English translation of the supplied one-page email printout. The text under “Source document” is the Court Registry’s notification; editorial context is separately identified below.

Source document - PDF page 1

Subject: Notification, internal number 25246-1/26
From: “Milanka Rankovic” milanka.rankovic@vrh.sud.rs
To: contact@origindossier.org
Date displayed: October 20, 2026 at 1:59 PM

Dear Sir or Madam,

In response to your request of 18 October 2026, pursuant to Article 223, paragraph 3, of the Court Rules of Procedure, we inform you that the appeal on points of law lodged against the Court of Appeal in Novi Sad decision Gž2 211/26, in connection with the Basic Court in Novi Sad decision P2 1435/24, has been registered with the Supreme Court under case number Rev 6599/26. At the same time, we inform you that your submission has been forwarded to the reporting judge for information.

Yours faithfully,

Deputy Head of the Court Registry
Milanka Ranković

Editorial context - not part of the notification

The email expressly responds to the author’s inquiry of 18 October 2026. It confirms registration of the appeal on points of law dated 29 May 2026 against decision Gž2 211/26, connected with the first-instance judgment P2 1435/24. The notification’s internal number is not the judicial case number.

The separately preserved SAPS portal printout records receipt and assignment on 13 July 2026 and a submission entry dated 19 October 2026. Those dates are retained as displayed; the portal entry is not rewritten as the author’s sending date of 18 October.

Limits of this confirmation: forwarding for information does not establish that the judge read the submission, visited Origin Dossier or formally incorporated the archive into the case file. The email is an administrative notification, not a decision on the merits or a statement of the appeal’s outcome. Article 223(3) is reproduced as cited, without an added interpretation. The printout provides no time zone.
