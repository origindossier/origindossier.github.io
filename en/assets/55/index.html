Milorad Vasić consent - false parental position from the 1979 void adoption record

Milorad Vasic Consent Record for Second Adoption (1984)

Page 1

Drawn up on the premises of the Centre for Social Work of the City of Novi Sad, on 12 September 1984, in the matter of giving consent for the adoption of the minor VASIĆ MILAN.

The summoned person, VASIĆ MILORAD, appears, aged 42, by occupation painter and decorator, married, father of two children, residing in Futog, Rade Kondića 17, and gives the following statement:

“I agree to give my minor son Vasić Milan, born on 28 June 1974 in Novi Sad, for adoption to a good family.

I consider this to be best for the child, because his mother Vasić Gospava died in April 1983, and I have established a new marital union in which my wife and I had a female child, the minor Dragana.

My wife Ratka did not tolerate Milan, so I was forced to place him with a foster family in Irig, because that was the only way to preserve my new marital union.

Since the child was placed with the foster family in Irig, which was in August 1983, I have maintained no contact with the child.

For the stated reasons, I think it will be very beneficial for the minor Milan that he has found parents and a family for himself.

I do not wish to be present at the act of adoption.

I have nothing further to say. The record was read to me during dictation, and I confirm my statement with my signature.”

Heard by:
Vera Radovanović

Recording clerk:
Lidija Šokić

Statement given by:
Vasić Milorad
