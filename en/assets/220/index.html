Hungarian Prime Minister’s Office: notice of referral to the competent ministry

Hungarian Prime Minister’s Office: notice of referral to the competent ministry

Full translation of the supplied one-page official email dated 24 August 2026, including its footer. The message records referral for further handling; it does not decide the substantive questions in the author’s case. Institution names and the original registration label are retained alongside their translations.

Source PDF

Source page 1

24 August 2026 at 12:54 PM

Subject: Transfer on grounds of competence – Fw: Reminder and update: documented case of Hungarian paternal identity erasure in Serbia

From: “Érkeztetők” erkeztetok@me.gov.hu
To: office@heriteon.com
File registration number (Iktatószám): ID_ME-IF/332/2/2026

Dear Milan Lakatoš Petrović,

We hereby inform you that, pursuant to Government Decree 90/2026 (V.13.) on the duties and powers of members of the Government, we are forwarding your inquiry received by the Prime Minister’s Office (Miniszterelnökség), on grounds of competence, to the Ministry Responsible for Social Relations and Culture (Társadalmi Kapcsolatokért és Kultúráért Felelős Minisztérium) for further handling.

Respectfully,

Prime Minister’s Office (Miniszterelnökség)
