Exclusion of Milivoj Miletić from O.196/79 — Pervaz appeal, adoption secrecy and court legalization of contaminated material

Appeal against the inheritance ruling in case O-196/79

Document type: Appeal
Court addressed: Municipal Court in Novi Sad
Case reference: O-196/79
Date of challenged ruling: 23 November 1979
Page: 1

Kopljan Rože of Novi Sad, 25 Zadružna Street,
Beker Irene of Novi Sad, 3 Trg Majke Jevrosime, and
the minor Ištvan Lakatoš, represented by his mother and legal guardian Verona Lakatoš
of Novi Sad, 3 Trg Majke Jevrosime,
represented by:

Jovan Pervaz
Attorney-at-law
Novi Sad, 6 Matice Srpske Street
Tel. 42-589

Against the inheritance ruling no. O-196/79 of 23 November 1979.
In 2 copies.
Attachment: power of attorney.

Against that part of the inheritance ruling of this court dated 23 November 1979, no. O-196/79, by which the minor Milivoj Miletić was declared an heir of the late Pavle Lakatoš, formerly of Novi Sad, and by which one quarter of the estate was awarded to him, we hereby submit this appeal within the statutory time limit and request that the court forward it, together with the entire case file, to the Higher Court in Novi Sad as the appellate court, which we ask to set aside the challenged part of the ruling and return the matter to the court of first instance for renewed consideration and a new decision.

This appeal is submitted on the grounds of a substantial violation of the rules of non-contentious procedure, incorrectly and incompletely established facts, and incorrect application of substantive law.

Reasons

First of all, we point out that the minor Milivoj Miletić cannot inherit the property of the late Pavle Lakatoš, formerly of Novi Sad, given that the statutory preconditions under the Inheritance Law of SAP Vojvodina have not been fulfilled, because the said person does not belong to the circle of statutory or testamentary heirs.

From the extract from the birth register, ref. no. 2512/74, dated 9 December 1974, it was established that Milivoj Miletić, born 28 June 1974, had Cvita Miletić listed as his mother, while the section containing data on the father remained blank, as did the section concerning subsequent entries and annotations.

Continuation

It is therefore clear that, in the specific case at hand, this is not an adopted child, and accordingly the minor Milivoj Miletić cannot claim inheritance rights as an adopted child.

Apart from this, no other conditions exist for the minor Milivoj to have been declared an heir. Namely, before the SIZ of Social Welfare, acting as the guardianship authority, proceedings were conducted for the full adoption of the minor Milivoj Miletić by another family. In those proceedings, the prospective adoptive parents declared that they did not seek for the minor Milivoj to inherit from anyone, but rather that he was to be regarded, as provided in the case of full adoption, as their child. In those proceedings it was further established that the late Pavle Lakatoš, formerly of Novi Sad, was not the natural father of the minor Milivoj Miletić, but only that there had existed on his part an intention to adopt this child, which he never carried out.

Evidence: Files of the SIZ Social Welfare / guardianship authority in Novi Sad, which we request the court to obtain ex officio.

On the basis of everything stated above, we consider our appeal well-founded, and therefore request that the court rule as set out in the prayer for relief of this appeal.

Novi Sad, 12 December 1979

Kopljan Roža et al.

Appellate ruling in case Gž.11/80

Document type: Appellate court ruling
Court: Higher Court in Novi Sad
Case reference: Gž.11/80
Related first-instance case: O.196/79
Date of session: 9 January 1980
Page: 1

The Higher Court in Novi Sad, acting as a second-instance civil court, sitting in a panel composed of judges Vladimir Dutina as presiding judge, and Dragan Torin and Vladimir Turčen as panel members, in the probate matter of the late Pavle Lakatoš, formerly of Novi Sad, deciding upon the appeal of the heirs Roža Kopljan, Irene Beker, and the minor Ištvan Lakatoš, represented by his mother Verona Lakatoš, all represented by Jovan Pervaz, attorney from Novi Sad, against the ruling of the Municipal Court in Novi Sad no. O.196/79 of 23 November 1979, at a closed session of the panel held on 9 January 1980, rendered the following

Ruling

The appeal is upheld, and the ruling of the Municipal Court in Novi Sad no. O.196/79 of 23 November 1979 is set aside in the contested part in which it was determined that the minor Milivoj Miletić is an heir of the deceased and that his hereditary share amounts to 1/4 of the estate, and the case is remanded to the court of first instance for renewed consideration and decision.

Reasoning

By the designated ruling, the court of first instance established the scope of the decedent's estate and the circle of heirs, and awarded to Roža Kopljan, Irene Beker, the minor Ištvan Lakatoš, and the minor Milivoj Miletić 1/4 of the estate each.

By a timely appeal, the heirs Roža Kopljan, Irene Beker, and the minor Ištvan Lakatoš challenge the said ruling in the part in which the minor Milivoj Miletić was declared an heir of the decedent and awarded a share of the estate, and they propose that it be set aside. They point out that Milivoj does not fall within the circle of statutory heirs, because he is neither the child nor the adopted child of the decedent, which they seek to prove by an extract from the birth register, in which the section for the father was not filled in. Furthermore, they argue...

Continuation

...that the decedent had only intended to adopt him, and that his death did not prevent this, but that circumstance was not directly examined by the competent SIZ for Social Welfare.

The appeal is well-founded.

The basic precondition for statutory inheritance concerns the degree of kinship between the decedent and the heir. A person claiming a right to inherit, or to a portion of the estate, is in the first place obliged to prove that he or she is related to the decedent, that is, that he or she is entitled to inherit by law. In the present case, the decedent's heirs are his sister Roža Kopljan, Irena Beker, and the son, the minor Ištvan Lakatoš. According to the extract from the birth register and the acts referred to in the file, the decedent's statutory heir may also be his illegitimate son, the minor Milivoj Miletić, whom the decedent allegedly acknowledged as his child. As to these claims of counsel, the other heirs did not make a statement, nor did the court of first instance otherwise conclusively determine the degree of kinship between Milivoj and the decedent; instead, it left in doubt whether he was the child or the adopted child of the decedent and therefore whether he was a statutory heir and whether he could inherit or receive a hereditary share of the estate. This all the more so because the appeal asserts that the decedent was neither the acknowledged father of the minor Milivoj nor his adoptive parent.

In view of the foregoing, the court of second instance upheld the appeal and set aside the contested part of the ruling.

In the renewed proceedings, the court of first instance shall require the temporary guardian of the minor Milivoj Miletić to provide evidence regarding the degree of kinship, that is, the existence of paternity between the decedent and the minor. In that regard, evidence shall be obtained as to whether the decedent recognized Milivoj as his child before the registrar, in a public instrument, or in a will, or whether parenthood was established by a final court judgment, or otherwise in the manner prescribed by law. Thereafter, the court shall again assess whether Milivoj may inherit from the estate and shall render a new decision in the part that was set aside.

In Novi Sad, on 9 January 1980

Presiding judge of the panel
Vladimir Dutina

Inheritance ruling in case O.28/80 — page 1

Document type: Inheritance ruling
Court: Municipal Court in Novi Sad
Case reference: O.28/80
Date: 10 November 1980
Page: 1

The Municipal Court in Novi Sad, by Judge Rajka Sujić, in the probate matter of the late Pavle Lakatoš (son of Stevan), former farmer, after conducting the probate proceedings, issues the following

Inheritance ruling

I. Estate of the deceased

After the late Pavle Lakatoš (son of Stevan), former farmer, born in 1928, divorced, citizen of the SFRY, with last residence at 5 Trg Majke Jevrosime, Novi Sad, who died on 7 November 1978 in Bački Vinogradi, without a will, the following property remained:

  1. In full ownership, real property recorded in possession sheet no. 800, cadastral municipality Bački Vinogradi: parcel no. 2700, vineyard, Roža Majur, area 34 ares 44 m²; parcel no. 2701, vineyard, Roža Majur, area 63 ares 38 m²; parcel no. 2699/1, house, yard and vineyard, Roža Majur, area 8 ares 58 m².

  2. In full ownership, extra-land-register real property recorded in possession sheet no. 407, cadastral municipality Novi Sad IV: parcel no. 578/1, field, area 11 ares 42 m²; parcel no. 578/2, field, area 11 ares 60 m²; parcel no. 528, field, area 8 ares 91 m²; parcel no. 629, field, area 33 ares 94 m².

  3. Extra-land-register real property in a 1/2 share, recorded in possession sheet no. 3160, cadastral municipality Novi Sad I: parcel no. 6244, area 8 ares 24 m² — house, yard and field adjoining the house.

  4. In full ownership, extra-land-register real property in possession sheet no. 1951, cadastral municipality Futog: parcel no. 9184, lower-land field, area 24 ares 91 m²; parcel no. 9185, lower-land field, area 16 ares 83 m²; parcel no. 9186, lower-land field, area 18 ares 04 m².

  5. One Fiat 1100 van, registration no. NS 325-65.

  6. One motor cultivator (milling / fruit-cultivation machine).

Liabilities

The liabilities of the estate amount to 60,000.00 dinars, which amount the deceased owed to Julijana Vizmeg of Bački Vinogradi, 23 Vinogradska Street.

II. Heirs called to inherit by law

On the legal basis of statutory inheritance, the heirs of the first order called to inherit from the deceased are:

  1. Roža KOPLJAN (daughter of Pavle), née Lakatoš, daughter,
    Novi Sad, Slana Bara, Zadružna no. 25

  2. Irena BEKER (daughter of Pavle), née Lakatoš, daughter,
    Novi Sad, 3 Trg Majke Jevrosime

  3. Minor Ištvan LAKATOŠ (son of Pavle), son,
    represented by Verona Lakatoš of Novi Sad, 3 Trg Majke Jevrosime,
    and those heirs are represented by attorney Jovan Pervaz of Novi Sad.

Continued on next page.

III. Heirs declared to inherit and transfer of title

With respect to the estate described under item I, on the legal basis of statutory inheritance, the following are declared heirs:

  1. Roža KOPLJAN (daughter of Pavle), née Lakatoš — 1/3 share
  2. Irena BEKER (daughter of Pavle), née Lakatoš — 1/3 share
  3. Minor Ištvan LAKATOŠ (son of Pavle) — 1/3 share

IV. Transfer of ownership

The land registry department of the Municipal Court in Novi Sad and the Municipal Court in Subotica are instructed, upon the finality of this ruling, to transfer ownership rights from the name of the decedent to the declared heirs.

Reasoning

On the basis of the death certificate of the Municipality of Novi Sad dated 18 December 1978, probate proceedings were initiated concerning the late Pavle Lakatoš, formerly of Novi Sad.

The estate left by the deceased consists of the assets and liabilities described under item I of this ruling.

In this probate matter, an inheritance ruling no. O.196/79 dated 23 November 1979 had already been issued. Under that ruling, in addition to the heirs listed under item II of this ruling, the minor Milivoj Miletić had also been declared an heir, whose temporary guardian was attorney Miloš Lisica of Novi Sad. The minor Milivoj Miletić had been reported as the illegitimate son of the decedent.

Against that ruling, the heirs Roža Kopljan, Irena Beker, and the minor Ištvan Lakatoš, through their representative attorney Jovan Pervaz of Novi Sad, filed an appeal. By the ruling of the Higher Court in Novi Sad, case no. Gž.11/80, dated 9 January 1980, the inheritance ruling of this court no. O.196/79 dated 23 November 1979 was set aside in the contested part in which it had been determined that the minor Milivoj Miletić was also an heir of the decedent.

In the repeated proceedings, it was established that the minor Milivoj Miletić is not the son of the decedent, nor had he been adopted by him, according to the report of the SIZ for Social Protection, Novi Sad, no. Up.03-608/79 dated 20 October 1980.

The daughters of the decedent, Roža Kopljan and Irena Beker, as well as the representative of the minor Ištvan Lakatoš, Verona Lakatoš, accepted the inheritance on the basis of law. With respect to the liabilities, the heirs recognized them in full in favor of Julijana Vizmeg.

On the basis of the foregoing, the court decided as stated in the operative part of the ruling.

The inheritance tax will be determined subsequently.

Instruction on legal remedy

A dissatisfied party has the right to appeal this ruling to the Higher Court in Novi Sad within 15 days from the date of receipt, through this court.

Judge
[signature]
Rajka Sujić, signed by hand
