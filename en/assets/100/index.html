Hearing postponed for lack of procedural conditions — P2.470/24, 2 April 2024

Record of the main hearing — P2.470/24, 2 April 2024

Source

This is a complete English translation of the two-page hearing record from the supplied photographs. The public PDF is a redacted copy; every occurrence of the plaintiff’s lawyer’s name is marked [redacted]. The judge, lay judges and court clerk remain named in their official roles.

The record gives a starting time of 08:15, postponement because the procedural conditions for holding the hearing were not met, and a closing time of 08:30. The plaintiff did not attend in person, his authorized representative did, and no one attended for the defendant; the case file did not show service of the defendant’s summons. The next hearing was scheduled for 24 April 2024 at 11:00. That date does not establish that the next hearing took place.

Editorial note

This note is not part of the hearing record. The statement that service upon the defendant is not shown does not signify deliberate refusal to attend. The preprinted small-claims notice is reproduced as form text, not as an additional classification of these proceedings. The record contains no decision on the merits of the claim; it records postponement, the new hearing date, and directions for notification and summons.

ZPP — Article 118 · Reading and correcting the hearing record

                
The form’s printed reference to “particulars under Article 118(1)” does not match the current Act’s numbering. Those particulars are in Article 116(1), to which the current small-claims provision, Article 474, expressly refers. The printed numbering corresponds to Articles 118(1) and 473 of the 2004 Act. This is evidence of an outdated reference in the form; it does not establish that the court applied the former Act or classified this identity/adoption dispute as a small claim.
