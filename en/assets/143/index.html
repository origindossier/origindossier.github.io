Author’s account: arrival on 5 June 2025, oral explanation and acceptance of 3 September

Author’s account — arrival on 5 June 2025 and the next date of 3 September

The author states that he came to the court on 5 June 2025 at the scheduled time. The judge told him orally that the CSR’s lawyer “had broken her leg”. No evidence of that reason was shown to him. He agreed to 3 September 2025 as the next hearing date and received no written document concerning that change.

This is the author’s separately provided account. The written record of 26 March 2025 had scheduled 5 June at 10:00; the oral explanation and the September date above come from his account, not from a new court record.
