M I N U T E S  
On Full Adoption

Drawn up on 21 June 1979 on the premises of the Self-Managing Community of Interest of Social Protection Novi Sad, as the authorized Guardianship Authority within the meaning of Article 1 of the Decision on Entrusting the Affairs of the Guardianship Authority to the Self-Managing Community of Interest of Novi Sad, Social Protection, /"Official Gazette of the Municipality of Novi Sad", No. 8/77/, in the matter of the full adoption of the minor MILETIĆ MILIVOJ, born on 28.06.1974 in Novi Sad, by the adopters VASIĆ MILORAD and VASIĆ GOSPAVA from Budisava, residing at Petefi Šandora Street No. 52.

Commenced at 8 o'clock.

Present at the hearing on full adoption:

1. Gamber Andja, officer of the Guardianship Authority,  
2. Molnar Lidija, recording clerk,  
3. Vasić Milorad, adopter,  
4. Vasić Gospava, adopter,  
5. Vera Radovanović, special guardian.

The official Gamber Andja states that all summoned persons are present at the hearing, that the same was scheduled at the request of Vasić Milorad and Vasić Gospava from Budisava, as adopters, for the purpose of the full adoption of the minor MILETIĆ MILIVOJ, and in accordance with the provision of Article 30 of the Adoption Act /"Official Gazette of SAP Vojvodina", No. 24/76/.

In the preceding procedure, the Guardianship Authority established that all legal conditions for full adoption had been met, and that this adoption is in accordance with the provisions of Articles 2 and 4 of the Adoption Act.

The mother of the minor Miletić Milivoj has for a longer period been at an unknown place of residence and, without justified reasons, has not been performing her parental duty, and for those reasons, and pursuant to Article 5 of the Adoption Act, in respect of the minor Miletić Milivoj, by Decision No. Up.03-506/79 of 20.06.79, a special guardian was appointed in the person of Vera Radovanović, social worker, who gives consent to this adoption.

It was established that there are no impediments under Articles 10 and 11, and a positive opinion under Article 15 of the Adoption Act was given.

Since the parties have been acquainted with the rights and duties of the adopters and the adoptee, the adopters agree that the adoptee shall inherit from their possible property and exercise all rights under Article 36 of the Adoption Act, and having been acquainted with the provision of Article 40 of the same Act, the adopters expressed the wish that the adoptee bear the family name of his adopters and be named VASIĆ MILAN.

Since all legal conditions for full adoption have been met, the Guardianship Authority:

P R O C L A I M S

That the full adoption has been established by which VASIĆ MILORAD, born on 15 February 1941 in the place of Osječani, Municipality of Doboj, and VASIĆ GOSPAVA, born on 21 August 1932 in the place of D. Klakar, Municipality of Doboj, both residing in Budisava, Petefi Šandora Street No. 52, ADOPT THE MINOR MILETIĆ MILIVOJ.

The adopted minor Miletić Milivoj shall hereafter be named VASIĆ MILAN.

The report on the full adoption shall be delivered by the Guardianship Authority to the competent registrar for the purpose of entering the adopters' particulars as the parents of the minor Miletić Milivoj.

The Self-Managing Community of Interest of Social Protection Novi Sad, as the authorized Guardianship Authority, pursuant to the entrustment of guardianship affairs to the Self-Managing Community of Interest of Social Protection Novi Sad /"Official Gazette of the Municipality of Novi Sad", No. 8/77/, and Article 9 paragraph 2 of the Law on Civil Registers /"Official Gazette of the SFRY", No. 8/65/, and Article 4 paragraph 3 of the Law on Civil Registers /"Official Gazette of the SRS", No. 46/74/, and Article 35 of the Adoption Act /"Official Gazette of SAPV", No. 24/76/, deciding upon the request of Vasić Milorad and Vasić Gospava from Budisava, in the matter of the subsequent entry of the adopters' particulars as parents in the register of births kept by the Municipality of Novi Sad, hereby issues:

R U L I N G

APPROVES the subsequent entry in the register of births of the fully adopted child, the minor MILETIĆ MILIVOJ, born on 28 June 1974 in Novi Sad, with the following parental particulars:

Particulars of the father:

VASIĆ MILORAD, citizen of the SFRY, born on 15 February 1941 in the place of Osječani, Municipality of Doboj, residing in Budisava, Petefi Šandora Street 52.

Particulars of the mother:

VASIĆ, née Tomić, GOSPAVA, citizen of the SFRY, born on 21 August 1932 in the place of D. Klakar, Municipality of Doboj, residing in Budisava, Petefi Šandora Street No. 52.

S t a t e m e n t   o f   R e a s o n s

Vasić Milorad and Vasić Gospava from Budisava, Petefi Šandora Street No. 52, submitted a request to this Authority for the subsequent entry in the register of births of the FULLY ADOPTED minor Miletić Milivoj, born on 28 June 1974 in Novi Sad, and for the entry of their particulars as parental particulars.

In the said request, the applicants stated that they request this because on 21.06.1979 they fully adopted the minor Miletić Milivoj according to the minutes on full adoption under No. Up.03-507/79, and that, pursuant to Articles 34 and 35 of the Adoption Act, they wish for the previous particulars in the register of births to be deleted and theirs entered, so that the adoptee shall henceforth bear the family name of his adopters and be named VASIĆ MILAN.

Upon consideration of this request, it was established that the same is justified, and therefore, within the meaning of Article 9 of the Law on Civil Registers, it was decided as set out in the operative part.

An appeal against this ruling may be lodged within 15 days from the day of receipt thereof to the Provincial Secretariat for Health and Social Policy of SAPV, directly or through this Community.

The appeal is exempt from fees.
