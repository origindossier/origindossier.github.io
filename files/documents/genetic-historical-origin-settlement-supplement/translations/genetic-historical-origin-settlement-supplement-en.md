## Later author’s clarification — known family relationships and personal contact

The author explains that the same DNA profile shows matches with the known paternal Lakatos branch and the maternal Kučić and Kamber branches, rather than merely with a person bearing the surname Lakatos. His mother’s mother’s maiden surname was Kamber. The Kučić relative shown is the son of the author’s maternal uncle’s daughter: a man surnamed Kučić married that daughter, and the displayed match is their son. Here, maternal uncle means the author’s mother’s brother, not his father’s brother. The author states that he has also met some of the relatives shown in person, without specifying here which of them he met. This is his later explanation of the family relationships; the report’s original wording and the platform’s displayed probable-relationship labels remain separate.
