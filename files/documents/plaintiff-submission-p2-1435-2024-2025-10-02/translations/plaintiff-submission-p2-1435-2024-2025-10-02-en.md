BASIC COURT IN NOVI SAD  
Case number: P2.1435/24

### PLAINTIFF’S SUBMISSION

(Objection concerning service and request for evidence justifying the defendant’s absence)

Plaintiff: Milan Petrović, Kragujevac, street and house number [redacted]  
Defendant: Centre for Social Work of the City of Novi Sad, Zmaj Ognjena Vuka 13, Novi Sad

### I. Relevant facts

- The hearing for my examination was scheduled for 3 September 2025 at 10:00.
- That morning I saw no notice for this case on the court’s noticeboard; after enquiring at the court, I was informed that the hearing had been postponed because of “health reasons affecting the defendant’s representative”.
- The court’s order/notice is dated 28 August 2025, bears a Novi Sad postal postmark of 2 September 2025, and was delivered to me on 4 September 2025 (a photocopy of the envelope is attached).
- The defendant’s request of 27 August 2025 (no. 560-111-1/2024) refers to the representative’s illness, but is not accompanied by any medical documentation.

This sequence — postal postmark 2 September → delivery 4 September, after the hearing date of 3 September — objectively calls into question both the timeliness of service and the justification for the postponement.

### II. Proposals and requests to the court

**1. Evidence of the defendant’s representative’s inability to attend**

That the court require the defendant, within seven days of receipt of this order, to provide a certificate from the competent doctor (healthcare institution, stamp, doctor’s name and signature, exact date and time of examination, and a clearly specified period of temporary inability to work/appear in court). To protect privacy, the diagnosis may be omitted; only evidence of actual inability to attend during the relevant period is required.

- If the document was created electronically, that the defendant also provide an extract from the system/record of the certificate’s issuance (log, number and date of issuance).
- That the court prohibit the defendant from destroying the original until the proceedings have concluded.

**2. Consequences if there is no evidence**

If the defendant fails to provide the requested certificate within the time limit, or provides incomplete/inadequate records, that the court refuse to accept the excuse and treat the absence as unjustified, with all procedural consequences under the Code of Civil Procedure (cost consequences, a warning concerning abuse of rights, unhindered taking of evidence with the attending party, and so forth).

**3. Dispatch records (court registry)**

That the court obtain and place in the case file the court registry’s official dispatch record (the date and time of outgoing dispatch of the notice/order dated 28 August 2025), so that the circumstances of timely service can be fully clarified.

### Editorial distinction between the two texts

The CSR request gives illness of an unnamed representative and the other representatives’ commitments as its reasons. The one-page court order states the cancellation and the next date; it contains no separate statement of reasons. The request and attached court order contain no medical certificate. The receipt stamp records the CSR request’s receipt by the court, not the plaintiff’s receipt of the court order.
