## Editorial context - not part of the notification

The email expressly responds to the [author’s inquiry of 18 October 2026](/en/documents/supreme-court-revision-status-inquiry-2026-10-18). It confirms registration of the [appeal on points of law dated 29 May 2026](/en/documents/supreme-court-revision-p2-1435-2024-2026-05-29) against [decision Gž2 211/26](/en/documents/appellate-judgment-gz2-211-2026-2026-04-22), connected with the [first-instance judgment P2 1435/24](/en/documents/first-instance-judgment). The notification’s internal number is not the judicial case number.

The [separately preserved SAPS portal printout](/en/documents/supreme-court-revision-saps-status-2026-10-20) records receipt and assignment on 13 July 2026 and a submission entry dated 19 October 2026. Those dates are retained as displayed; the portal entry is not rewritten as the author’s sending date of 18 October.

**Limits of this confirmation:** forwarding for information does not establish that the judge read the submission, visited Origin Dossier or formally incorporated the archive into the case file. The email is an administrative notification, not a decision on the merits or a statement of the appeal’s outcome. Article 223(3) is reproduced as cited, without an added interpretation. The printout provides no time zone.
