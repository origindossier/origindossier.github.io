# Second Adoption Record - Incomplete Adoption (1984)

### Record of Adoption

Centre for Social Work  
of the City of Novi Sad  
No.: Up.02-987/84  
Date: 26 September 1984  
Novi Sad

Drawn up on 26 September 1984 on the premises of the Centre for Social Work of the City of Novi Sad, as the authorised guardianship authority pursuant to Article 3 of the Law on Guardianship of SAP Vojvodina, No. 24/71, and Article 1 of the Decision on Entrusting Guardianship Affairs to the Centre for Social Work in Novi Sad by the Municipal Assemblies of Stari Grad, Liman, Podunavlje, Detelinara, Slavija, Petrovaradin and Sremski Karlovci (“Official Gazette of the City of Novi Sad”, Nos. 21/81, 7/83, 6/83, 13/83, 9/83, 7/85), in the matter of the adoption of the minor VASIĆ MILAN, born on 28 June 1974 in Novi Sad, by the adopters PETROVIĆ PERIŠA and PETROVIĆ GORGINA from Kragujevac, Kraljevački bataljon Street 110.

Commenced at 10:00.

The following persons are present at the adoption hearing:

1. GAMBER ANĐA, officer of the guardianship authority,  
2. JASMINA JOVIČIĆ, recording clerk,  
3. PETROVIĆ PERIŠA, adopter,  
4. PETROVIĆ GORGINA, adopter,  
5. VASIĆ MILAN, adoptee.

The official person, Anđa Gamber, establishes that all summoned persons have appeared at the hearing, that the hearing was scheduled at the request of the adopters Periša Petrović and Gorgina Petrović from Kragujevac, for the purpose of adopting the minor VASIĆ MILAN, in accordance with Article 4 of the Law on Adoption of SAP Vojvodina (“Official Gazette of SAPV”, No. 24/76).

In the preparatory procedure, within the meaning of Article 14 of the Law on Adoption, the guardianship authority established that all statutory conditions for this adoption had been fulfilled, and that there were no obstacles under Article 2 of the Law on Adoption.

It was also established that there were no obstacles under Article 6 of the Law on Adoption, and since the minor VASIĆ MILAN is older than 10 years, he gives his consent to this adoption.

It was also established that there were no obstacles under Articles 10 and 11, and a positive opinion under Article 15 of the same Law was given.

The father of the minor VASIĆ MILAN gave consent for the adoption on 12 September 1984 on the premises of the Centre for Social Work of the City of Novi Sad, and also stated that he did not wish to attend the adoption hearing; for those reasons he was not summoned to the hearing.

The adopters were specifically informed of the provision of Article 20 of the Law on Adoption, and stated that they agreed that the minor VASIĆ MILAN would inherit them equally from their possible estate.

Having been informed of the provision of Article 21, the adopters expressed the wish that the minor VASIĆ MILAN should henceforth use their family surname, and that in the future he should use the personal name PETROVIĆ MILAN.

It is established that all statutory conditions for adoption have been fulfilled, and therefore the guardianship authority:

### DECLARES

That the adoption has been established, whereby PETROVIĆ PERIŠA, citizen of the SFRY,

born on 14 November 1925 in Pajsijević - Knić, and PETROVIĆ, née Miljković, GORGINA, citizen of the SFRY, born on 22 July 1935 in Toponica, both residing in Kragujevac, Kraljevački bataljon Street 110, adopt the minor VASIĆ MILAN.

The adopted minor VASIĆ MILAN shall henceforth use the personal name PETROVIĆ MILAN.

The guardianship authority shall deliver the adoption report to the competent registrar for entry of the necessary data.

Concluded at 10:30.
