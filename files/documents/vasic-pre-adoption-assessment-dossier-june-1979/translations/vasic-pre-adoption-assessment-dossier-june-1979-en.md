### Report of the Social Worker — On the suitability of Vasić Gospava and Milorad for the adoption of a child

The married couple VASIĆ GOSPAVA and MILORAD live in Budisava, Petefi Šandora Street No. 52.

VASIĆ GOSPAVA, born on 21 August 1932 in Donji Klakar near Doboj. Gospava's mother, Tomić Petra, a housewife by occupation, died in 1955. Her father, Tomić Jova, a farmer by occupation, was killed in 1942 in the camp at Jasenovac. Gospava grew up in a family with her mother and two sisters in Donji Klakar. Her sister Darinka, a housewife, married, mother of 4 children, lives in Novi Sad, and her sister Draginja, married, housewife, mother of 4 children, lives with her family in Istanbul.

Gospava completed primary school. She came to Novi Sad in 1946. She entered into her first marriage in 1954 and divorced in 1957, since she could not have children. She entered into marriage with Vasić Milorad on 21 January 1962. She was not in regular employment, but worked occasionally as a laborer. According to the medical certificate, she is physically and mentally healthy and capable of the adoption of a child.

Gospava is a mature personality, steady and persistent, ready to sacrifice herself in order to achieve harmonious and bearable relations with her nearer and wider surroundings. She possesses positive attitudes toward marriage and family. She has a tendency to live in an organized manner and to put all things in their place. She has a need for work and order, which she will be able to transfer to the child.

VASIĆ MILORAD, born on 15 February 1941 in Osječani near Doboj. His mother Velka, a housewife by occupation, lives in Osječani. His father Vasić Nedeljko, a farmer by occupation, died in 1974. Milorad grew up in the family of his parents with four brothers and three sisters.

### Report of the Education Specialist — On the suitability of Vasić Milorad and Gospava for the adoption of a child

The role of the education specialist in the case of Vasić Milorad and Gospava is to examine and consider the educational-upbringing conditions for the development of the child in the family of the candidate adopters. For the purpose of as complete an insight as possible into the needs of the child, a conversation was conducted with both spouses and a visit was made to the family.

The basic motive for adoption is the satisfaction of parental instinct, because spouses Vasić Milorad and Gospava, for health reasons, do not have children of their own. In addition, there is also the desire to provide a child without parental care with appropriate help and protection.

Vasić Milorad was born in 1941 in Osječani (Doboj), where he completed 4 grades of primary school. Later he moved to Novi Sad, where he entered into employment in the city greenery service as an unskilled worker. During his work he continued his education, acquired the qualification of a highly qualified worker, and today is employed at “Novograp” in the position of brigade leader.

Vasić Milorad is a diligent and conscientious worker. He relates responsibly toward his work obligations, and in the work collective he enjoys the reputation of a good companion and a diligent worker. In addition to obligations at the workplace, he devotes enough time to the family, participates in the organization of family life, and by his work at home contributes to the improvement of the material and other conditions of the family.

Milorad is open, communicative, and direct in contacts. He grew up and developed in a large family, which, among other things, contributed to a proper and selfless attitude toward others and a humane relation toward people in general. He is attached to his relatives and close friends; he can and knows how to love, to understand the problems of others, and to place himself properly toward them. He possesses an appropriate pedagogical culture for performing the function of adopter.

Vasić Gospava was born in 1932 in Gorenji Kalakar (Bos. Brod). She completed two grades of primary school.

---

## Page 16

### Report of the Education Specialist — Continuation and conclusion

Her schooling was interrupted because of the war, since the whole family was taken to a camp. Gospava had a difficult childhood; she lost both parents at an early age (killed in the camp), so that from the earliest days she acquired independence and worked in order to secure for herself means of living. By marrying Milorad, she terminated her employment, but by her work in the house, which she still does today, she contributed to the material status of the family.

Vasić Gospava is quiet, well-intentioned, and sincere. In her actions she is measured, spontaneous, tolerant, and very devoted to the family, with a strongly expressed desire to have a child, to care for it, and to give it love and attention. Gospava is very diligent, conscientious, and orderly.

The spouses Vasić have been living in marriage for about 18 years. The Vasić family leads an organized family life; harmony prevails in the family, and relations are harmonious. In the organization of family life there are harmonized attitudes, which will have a reflection on the positive upbringing of the child and the proper formation of his personality.

The minor Miletić Milivoje, the child whom the spouses Vasić wish to adopt, is placed with a foster family in Irig. Milivoj is a child without parental care; his father is unknown, and his mother is at an unknown place of residence. Milivoj was born on 28 June 1974 in Novi Sad. For his age the child is physically normally developed, healthy, and of adjusted behavior. Spouses Vasić Milorad and Gospava have established contact with the child; a certain emotional bond has been created, which becomes greater each day.

Taking into consideration all circumstances in a complex manner, with special reference to the educational-upbringing conditions for the development of the child in the family of the adopters, the education specialist is of the opinion that the adoption of the minor Miletić Milivoje by spouses Vasić Gospava and Milorad is justified and purposeful, that is, that there exist personal and social interests for the adoption to be concluded.

### Psychologist's Opinion — Examinees, methods, and findings

PSYCHOLOGIST'S OPINION

Examinees:

VASIĆ GOSPAVA, born on 21 August 1932 in Gornji Klakar, near Bosanski Brod. She has two grades of primary school. She worked for nine years as an unskilled worker in “Novkabel” in Novi Sad. She terminated her employment by agreement with her husband in order to devote herself to work in the house.

VASIĆ MILORAD, born on 15 February 1941 in Osječani. He has secondary vocational education. He works in the jobs of painter and decorator.

Vasić Gospava and Milorad have been in marriage for 18 years. They have no children. They expressed the wish to fully adopt a child.

Methods applied:

- interview
- observation
- TNR
- RPM
- Cornell index

Behavior during the examination

The examinees have insight into the purpose of the examination. From the very beginning, good contact was established and easily maintained. Preservation of the senses and motor skills is average. They accept all test situations, but in the case of Vasić Gospava it was not possible to apply verbal tests because the examinee does not know how to read. They are motivated for work and persistent. They maintain attention well. They assess their work realistically. The obtained data may be accepted as a reliable sample of the potentials of the examinees.

Intelligence and personality

Both examinees are of normal intellectual abilities and at the level designated as average intelligence.

In the case of Gospava, projective techniques were not applied for the stated reasons, and when it comes to Milorad no data were obtained to which some greater psychodiagnostic significance should be given. That is, Milorad has properly formed attitudes toward the family as a whole, toward superiors and subordinates, toward colleagues and friends. He has no pronounced neurotic tendencies.
