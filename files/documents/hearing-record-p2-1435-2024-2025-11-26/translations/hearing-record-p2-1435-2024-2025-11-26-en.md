## Editorial notes on the source

- The record repeatedly gives **29 October 2025** as the date of the plaintiff’s submission. The separately supplied submission in this dossier is dated **27 October 2025**. Both dates are preserved; this record does not explain the difference.
- On page 3, the ruling says **“the defendant’s submission”**, while the preceding passages on page 2 refer to the plaintiff’s submission. The translation preserves that wording.
- The small-claims instructions and the reference to Article 118 on page 1 are printed form text. They do not constitute a separate ruling classifying this action as a small claim.
- The rotated, clipped fragment of printed text at the upper-right edge of page 2 is incomplete and is not reconstructed as a separate statement. The supplied image preserves it.
